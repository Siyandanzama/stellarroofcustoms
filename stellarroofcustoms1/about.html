About Us

StellarRoof Customs was founded in Johannesburg to provide luxury car interior upgrades. We specialize in precision engineering, turning ordinary vehicle roofs into high-end celestial experiences.

Our Mission

Deliver high-quality custom lighting solutions with absolute meticulous craftsmanship, ensuring no damage to vehicle electrics and factory aesthetics.

Our Vision

Become South Africa's leading custom car interior brand, recognized across Gauteng and beyond for luxury, innovation, and client satisfaction.
